Silibus — Course Outline

14 weeks
Week 01–02

Kinematics & Dynamics

Motion in one and two dimensions, Newton's laws, applications.

Week 03

Work, Energy & Power

Conservation of energy, work-energy theorem.

Week 04–06

Rotational Mechanics

Moment of inertia, torque, rotational kinetic energy.

Week 07

Equilibrium & Centre of Mass

Rigid body equilibrium, beam and ladder problems.

Week 08

Angular Momentum

Conservation of angular momentum, rotational collisions.

Week 09–10

Intermolecular Forces

Lennard-Jones potential, stress-strain behaviour.

Week 11–13

AC Circuits

RLC circuits, impedance, resonance.

Week 14

Review & HOTS Assessment

Consolidation and higher-order problem sets.
